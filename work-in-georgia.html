The rules since 1 March 2026

Georgia now requires a work permit for almost every foreign worker. In short:

The employer appliesThe permit is tied to one employer and one position. The employer applies online and pays the state fee: 200 GEL (decision within 30 days) or 400 GEL (within 10 working days).
Local candidates firstThe employer first posts the job on the state portal. The state employment agency has 10 working days to suggest a Georgian candidate.
Work visa, even if you are visa-freeIf you are outside Georgia, you apply for a D1 work visa at a Georgian embassy after the permit is granted. Then you get a residence permit for work.
Some jobs are closedForeigners cannot work as couriers, taxi or passenger drivers, or tour guides (a small quota exists for mountain guides). We do not take these jobs.
Working without a permit is finedThe worker and the employer are each fined 2,000 GEL the first time, more after that.

Sources: JAMnews, OC Media, Law of Georgia on Labour Migration. Rules change; we check the current rules for your case.
