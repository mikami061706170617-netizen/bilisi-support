Your teachers

Chef Kyohei Mikami has cooked omurice in Japan since 2010 and won the Omurice Stadium 2019 Hokkaido tournament. He now runs Omurice Land inside Japan Food Hub, Tbilisi. Georgian classes are taught by our Georgian cooks from Japan Food Hub. Classes are in simple English, with Georgian and Russian help from our team.

Choose your class

Omurice

Our signature. Ketchup rice and a soft omelette you fold yourself — the chef's competition technique.

Japanese curry

Make the roux from scratch and fry a crispy katsu to go on top.

Ramen

Our broth is ready when you arrive. You make the tare, the soft-boiled egg and the toppings, then build your own bowl.

Khinkali

Georgia's famous soup dumplings. Mix the filling, roll the dough and learn the pleats — then eat them the Georgian way.

Khachapuri

Georgian cheese bread with melted cheese, baked fresh.

Sushi rolls

Season the rice and roll salmon and vegetable maki. Beginner friendly.

Japanese-style Western dishes (hamburg steak, Napolitan spaghetti) are available for private groups. Vegetarian versions possible — tell us when you book.
